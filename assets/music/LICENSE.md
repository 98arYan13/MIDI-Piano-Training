This collection contains five original simplified arrangements of public-domain historical musical themes and five original practice exercises: C Major Scale, A Minor Scale, C Major Arpeggios, Interval Steps and Five Finger Patterns. The arrangements, exercises, backing parts, chord maps and encodings are dedicated under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No reference project's files or assets were used.

`manifest.json` records the historical source and the precise independent arrangement method for every file. `scripts/generate-starters.ps1` reproduces the files byte for byte. These are beginner practice arrangements with intentionally regular rhythm; BWV 846 is a short simplified revoicing of its first four harmonies, not a complete score.

## Optional tutorial library

`tutorials.json` describes 83 additional lessons. MIDI files in `tutorials/` are generated or downloaded by `node scripts/setup-tutorials.mjs`, and are excluded from Git. Each catalog entry carries its exact source URL, SHA-256 and attribution.

- 16 original Foundations patterns and the independently authored practice guides are dedicated to CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
- 18 Burgmüller Op. 100 studies: public-domain Mutopia editions, typeset by Bas Wassink. Historical source: Collection Litolff, nineteenth century. Only the studies actually available from Mutopia are included; this is not the complete 25-study set.
- 10 Czerny Op. 840 studies: public-domain Mutopia editions, maintained by Manuela. Historical source: IMSLP; Mainz: Schott, n.d. [1855], plate 13253.
- 19 Czerny Op. 821 studies: public-domain Mutopia editions, maintained by Manuel Castejon Limas. Historical source: Leipzig: Edition Peters, n.d. [1888], plates 6990–6993. This is a selection from the 160 studies, not the full book.
- 20 Hanon Part I exercises: Charles-Louis Hanon, *The Virtuoso Pianist*, 1873; Schirmer, 1900; Mutopia typesetting by Steve Taylor and Javier Ruiz-Alma, Mutopia-2015/07/23-2037.
