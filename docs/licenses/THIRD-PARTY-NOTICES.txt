# Salamander Grand Piano v3

Samples by **Alexander Holm**, Yamaha C5, 48 kHz / 24-bit, 16 velocity layers.
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: [sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano), pinned revision `3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404`.

This application's `protected.sfz` is an independently generated mapping of these samples, informed by the natural tuning and key ranges in kinwie's reconstruction of the Salamander instrument. It removes keyswitches, per-note polyphony caps and choking so overlapping live notes and their release tails can be protected. Samples are unchanged. It includes string resonance and hammer release samples; sustain ownership is managed by the native engine.

The sample library, upstream mapping data and downloads are ignored by Git and provisioned by `scripts/setup-native.ps1`. Do not replace missing assets with synthetic sound. A complete checksum manifest is kept beside this attribution.

===== assets/ear/README.md =====
# Original interval reference motifs

The interval-reference phrases in native/src/EarFlash.cpp were independently authored for this application on 2026-10-12. Their musical content is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No reference-project code, assets, or copyrighted song quotations are included.

For each selected minor/major second, third, sixth or seventh, perfect fourth/fifth/octave or tritone, the motif alternates the chosen root and directed interval pitch for four equal quarter-note pulses, each held for 80% of its pulse. Transposition moves both pitches; reverse reverses the phrase. Harmonic presentation uses two repeated root+interval dyads, distinct from the single interval-pair dyad. Native tempo determines timing; the attributed Salamander piano supplies sound.
